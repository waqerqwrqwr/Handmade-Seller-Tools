About Simple Tool Works

Simple Tool Works is a small, independent project that builds no-fuss calculators and spreadsheet tools for people who sell things — handmade makers, craft-fair regulars, Etsy and eBay sellers, and anyone else who has to guess at a price and hope it works.

Why this site exists

Pricing small-business work is messier than it looks. Fees stack up in ways nobody explains up front, shipping quietly eats a margin, and two nearly identical shops can land on very different take-home numbers for the same $40 sale. Most of the time people aren't short on effort — they're short on a clean place to put the numbers and see what's actually left. That gap is the whole reason this site exists. We wanted a page you could open in ten seconds, type three numbers into, and walk away with a clearer answer.

What the tools are

Every tool here follows the same idea. It's free to use, needs no sign-up and no email address, and runs right in your browser. Nothing is hidden behind a login, and you don't have to hand over a card to see a result. The defaults you see are ordinary starting points, not official rates — they're meant to be overwritten with your own numbers. If your transaction fee or fixed fee differs, change it. The tool is a calculator, not an authority, and it works best when you feed it what's true for your shop.

What we try to hold to

Three things, mainly. First, no inflated claims — if a tool estimates fees, it says it estimates fees, and it doesn't pretend to know your real profit. Second, no invented numbers. When we show a default percentage, we label it plainly as an assumption rather than dressing it up as a fact. Third, official numbers win. Marketplace fees, payment processing rates, and tax rules change, often by country, so the rate that applies to you is the one printed by the source that charges it. When this site and the platform disagree, the platform is right.

What this site is not

Simple Tool Works is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Etsy, eBay, or any other marketplace or payment provider. We don't provide financial, accounting, or tax advice, and nothing here should be read as a substitute for a professional opinion tailored to your situation. Use the tools at your own discretion, and check your own account and local rules before making a decision that matters.

How the content changes

Pages get revised as tools and features change, so what you read today may be updated later without a formal announcement. Where a third-party service is involved — a marketplace, a payment processor, a storefront — its own terms, privacy policy, pricing, and processing rules apply alongside anything here. Questions about a specific product are best handled through the support route tied to that product, which the Contact page explains.
