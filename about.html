About Simple Tool Works

Simple Tool Works creates practical calculators and spreadsheet tools for independent sellers, makers, and small businesses.

The goal is simple: turn everyday pricing and profitability questions into tools that are easy to understand and quick to use. The free calculators are designed for planning and estimation. The Pro Excel products are intended for sellers who want a repeatable workflow for managing product costs and pricing decisions.
