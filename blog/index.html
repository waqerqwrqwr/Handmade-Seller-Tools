What this blog covers, and where to begin

These guides sit alongside the free calculators and explain the thinking behind them. Each article works through one question a handmade seller or small online business runs into, such as how to build a price, what profit actually means, or which costs are easy to forget, and shows how the numbers fit together.

What you will find here

Straightforward, practical articles on pricing, costs, fees, and profit. They assume you already make or sell something and want a clearer way to think about the money side. You will not find growth tactics or vague encouragement; the focus stays on the everyday arithmetic of running a small shop.

Who these guides are for

They are written for makers and resellers who set their own prices: craft sellers, Etsy-style shop owners, and anyone selling secondhand or made-to-order goods. If you have ever finished a sale and been unsure what you actually earned, these articles are aimed at you.

Where to start reading

If you are pricing your work for the first time, begin with the guide on pricing handmade products without losing money. If you already charge a price but want to see what is left after costs, read the article on calculating real profit. And if you suspect you have been underpricing out of habit, the mistakes checklist is the quickest place to check yourself. Each guide pairs with the calculator that matches it, so you can put the ideas to work straight away.
