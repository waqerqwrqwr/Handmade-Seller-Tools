Contact

Reaching us is simple, but which route you take depends on what you're asking about.

Product and purchase questions

If your question is about something you bought — a spreadsheet, a template, a paid download — please use the support channel on the product page where you bought it. Payhip storefronts include a message option tied to the order, and that is the fastest route because it keeps your order details, your receipt, and your question together in one thread. We can see exactly what you purchased and when, which usually means a shorter back-and-forth and far less guessing.

Website problems and feedback

For everything about the site itself — a calculator that returns something odd, a page that will not load on your phone, a typo, a broken link, or a suggestion for a tool you wish existed — use the general feedback route on the storefront or site. This covers the free tools, the layout, and anything that feels wrong or confusing. Honest complaints are welcome; they are how the tools get better.

What to include

A little detail saves a lot of time. It helps to know which page you were on, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened. If a number looked wrong, tell us the values you entered and the result you saw. Add your browser and device if the problem looks visual or only happens on one machine, and a screenshot if you have one. If it is about a purchase, include your order reference.

Before you write

Many questions are already answered on the page itself. The free tools carry short FAQs under the calculator, and the common ones cover how the numbers are worked out, what is included, and why a default is editable rather than official. Checking there first is usually the quickest fix.

How quickly we reply

We read everything and reply as soon as we reasonably can, but this is a small independent operation, not a staffed call centre. Please don't hold us to a specific turnaround, and don't build a deadline around an answer you haven't received yet. If it is genuinely urgent, say so in the first line and we will do our best to prioritise it.
