How to Estimate Etsy Seller Fees

What this Etsy fee calculator does not include

  • Product or material costs
  • Labor and packaging costs
  • Seller-paid shipping
  • Taxes or other costs not entered into the calculator

How this calculator works

Revenue after fees = sale price − transaction fee − processing fee − fixed fee

Example

On a $32 sale with 6.5% transaction fee, 3% processing, and a $0.25 fixed fee, estimated fees are about $3.29 and revenue after fees is about $28.71.

Important assumptions

Results are planning estimates. Replace the default assumptions with your actual costs, rates, shipping arrangements, discounts, and sales volume. Marketplace and payment fees can change over time.

How much does Etsy take from a sale?

The amount depends on the fee rates that apply to your shop and transaction. Enter the rates you want to model instead of assuming one universal total.

Can I use this for an Etsy pricing decision?

Yes. Use the fee estimate as one input in a pricing decision, then check it against your actual seller account and the other costs of fulfilling the order.

Can I use this for my own business?

Yes. Enter your own numbers to create a simple planning estimate. The calculators are designed for comparison and everyday decisions.

Are these official marketplace calculators?

No. They are independent planning tools and do not represent an official marketplace or payment provider.
