How to Price Products on Etsy

What to check before you publish an Etsy price

  • Your complete cost per item
  • Your seller-paid shipping and packaging costs
  • The fee rates that actually apply to your shop
  • The profit margin you want after selling costs

How this calculator works

Suggested price = (product cost + fixed fee) ÷ (1 − target margin − percentage fee rates)

Example

With $16.50 in product cost, 6.5% transaction fees, 3% processing, a $0.25 fixed fee, and a 30% target margin, the estimate is about $26.38, with about $7.91 profit.

Important assumptions

Results are planning estimates. Replace the default assumptions with your actual costs, rates, shipping arrangements, discounts, and sales volume. Marketplace and payment fees can change over time.

How do I calculate a selling price on Etsy?

Add your product cost and the fees you expect to pay, then work backward from your target profit margin. The calculator solves for a price that can cover those inputs.

Is this an official Etsy calculator?

No. This is an independent planning tool. Etsy fees can change, so verify the rates that apply to your account before using the result for a live listing.

Can I use this for my own business?

Yes. Enter your own numbers to create a simple planning estimate. The calculators are designed for comparison and everyday decisions.

Are these official marketplace calculators?

No. They are independent planning tools and do not represent an official marketplace or payment provider.
