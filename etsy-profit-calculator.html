How to Calculate Etsy Profit

Costs that can change Etsy profit

  • Materials and other product costs
  • Your labor cost
  • Packaging and seller-paid shipping
  • Marketplace or payment fees

How this calculator works

Profit = sale price − product costs − selling fees

Example

At a $30 sale, with $8 materials, $4 labor, $1 packaging, $1 seller-paid shipping, and $2.20 fees, estimated profit is $13.80 with a margin of about 46%.

Important assumptions

Results are planning estimates. Replace the default assumptions with your actual costs, rates, shipping arrangements, discounts, and sales volume. Marketplace and payment fees can change over time.

How do I calculate Etsy profit per sale?

Subtract your product costs and selling fees from the sale price. Include labor and seller-paid shipping when those costs are part of fulfilling the order.

What is a good Etsy profit margin?

There is no universal margin that fits every product or business. Use your own costs, time, pricing position, and goals to choose a margin that makes the product worth selling.

Can I use this for my own business?

Yes. Enter your own numbers to create a simple planning estimate. The calculators are designed for comparison and everyday decisions.

Are these official marketplace calculators?

No. They are independent planning tools and do not represent an official marketplace or payment provider.
