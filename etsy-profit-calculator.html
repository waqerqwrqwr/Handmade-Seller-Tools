How it works

Profit = sale price − product costs − seller-paid costs − percentage fees − fixed fees

Example

Suppose a product sells for $30. Materials cost $8, labor is $4, packaging is $1, and seller-paid shipping is $1. If estimated selling fees are $2.20, the estimated profit is $13.80, or about a 46% profit margin.

Important assumptions

Use your own costs and rates whenever possible. Results are estimates for planning and comparison, not financial, tax, legal, or marketplace advice.

What does this calculator estimate?

It uses the inputs you provide to estimate the selected pricing, fee, profit, margin, or break-even result. Change the assumptions to match your own business.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. The calculator is an estimate. Your actual costs, platform rules, payment fees, shipping arrangements, taxes, returns, discounts, and sales volume can change the result.

Can I use this for a different marketplace?

Yes, when the relevant rates and cost assumptions can be entered. The calculator does not claim to be an official marketplace calculator.
