How this calculator works

Total cost = materials + labor + packaging + shipping + overhead; markup price = total cost × (1 + markup)

Example

With $7 materials, 0.75 hours at $20/hour, $1.50 packaging, $3 shipping, and $1 overhead, total cost is $27.50. A 100% markup gives $55.00.

Important assumptions

Results are planning estimates. Replace the default assumptions with your actual costs, rates, shipping arrangements, discounts, and sales volume. Marketplace and payment fees can change over time.
