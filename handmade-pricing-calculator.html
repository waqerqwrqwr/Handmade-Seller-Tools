After you get a price, compare it with your market and your actual customer value. A profitable price is a starting point for a business decision, not a promise that the market will accept any number.

This calculator lets you compare a markup-based price with a margin-based price. That distinction matters: markup is calculated from cost, while margin is calculated from selling price.

The real cost of a handmade item is usually more than the material bill. Start with the materials, then add the cost of your production time, packaging, shipping you absorb, and a fair share of overhead.

How to Price Handmade Products

What to include in handmade product cost

  • Materials and supplies used in one item
  • Labor hours multiplied by your target hourly rate
  • Packaging and seller-paid shipping
  • Overhead allocated to each item

How this calculator works

Total cost = materials + labor + packaging + shipping + overhead; markup price = total cost × (1 + markup)

Example

With $7 materials, 0.75 hours at $20/hour, $1.50 packaging, $3 shipping, and $1 overhead, total cost is $27.50. A 100% markup gives $55.00.

Important assumptions

Results are planning estimates. Replace the default assumptions with your actual costs, rates, shipping arrangements, discounts, and sales volume. Marketplace and payment fees can change over time.

Should I include my labor when pricing handmade items?

Yes. Your production time is a real business cost. Enter the hours spent on the item and the hourly rate you want the business to pay you.

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup measures profit as a percentage of cost. Margin measures profit as a percentage of the selling price. The same item can have very different markup and margin percentages.

Can I use this for my own business?

Yes. Enter your own numbers to create a simple planning estimate. The calculators are designed for comparison and everyday decisions.

Are these official marketplace calculators?

No. They are independent planning tools and do not represent an official marketplace or payment provider.
