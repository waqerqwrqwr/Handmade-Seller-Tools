HOW IT WORKS

Three numbers decide whether a product is worth selling.

Every calculator here is built around the same short chain of questions. Work through them in order and a pricing decision stops being a guess.

01 — WHAT IT COSTS

Total the cost of one unit

Add materials, the labour hours you actually spend, packaging, the shipping you absorb, and any platform or payment fees. Most underpricing starts here, with a cost that was never counted in the first place.

02 — WHAT YOU KEEP

Subtract the fees that scale

Percentage fees rise with the price, so they belong inside the calculation rather than being bolted on afterwards. A five point difference in fees is a five point difference in what reaches your account.

03 — WHAT YOU NEED

Set the margin, then solve for price

Decide the profit you want to keep and let the calculator work backwards to the price that delivers it. If that price looks unrealistic, the answer is to cut cost or rethink the product, not to quietly drop your margin.

QUESTIONS

Common questions about the calculators.

Are the calculators free to use?

Yes. Every tool on the site runs in your browser, requires no account, and costs nothing. You can enter as many scenarios as you like and change any assumption to match your own business.

Are the fee rates official?

No. The rates we start you with are editable assumptions, not official quotes from any marketplace. Fees differ by country, category and account type, and they change over time, so check the platform's own fee page and type in the numbers that apply to you.

Do I need Excel or any other software?

No. The free calculators are ordinary web pages — a browser is all you need, on desktop or on a phone. The Pro Excel workbook is optional, and only useful if you want the same work kept in a reusable file.

Do the calculators store the numbers I enter?

The maths runs in your browser as you type. The figures you enter are not submitted to us and are not saved on our servers. Reloading the page clears them.

Which calculator should I start with?

If you are deciding what to charge, start with the pricing calculator. If you already have a price and want to know what survives fees, start with the fee or profit calculator. If you are planning an event or a launch, start with the break-even calculator.

What is the difference between the free tools and the Pro Excel workbook?

The free tools answer one question at a time and are designed to be quick. The workbook is for sellers who want the same calculations kept together — pricing, a product database, and wholesale numbers in one file you can return to.
