Privacy Policy

Simple Tool Works is a static informational website offering free browser-based calculators and optional digital products. This page explains what information is and is not handled when you visit the site, use a tool, or buy something. It describes the site's real practices rather than promising compliance with any named regulation.

Tools run in your browser

The calculators on this site are plain web pages that run entirely in your browser. The numbers and assumptions you type are processed locally on your own device. They are not transmitted to us, not stored on a server, and not kept after you close or refresh the page. The site has no user accounts, no login, and no sign-up forms that collect personal details, so there is no profile on our side to create, look up, or delete.

Advertising and cookies

The site displays advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, including personalized advertising based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can review and adjust how Google personalizes ads through Google's own ad settings, and you can block or clear cookies in your browser. Where local rules require it, ad partners may present consent choices before using certain identifiers. Data gathered for advertising is handled under Google's policies.

Payments and digital products

Any paid digital product or download is sold through Payhip, a third-party checkout platform. Payments are processed by Payhip and its payment providers; we do not receive or store your card number or full payment details. Receipts, refunds, file delivery, and the processing of payment information are governed by Payhip's own terms and privacy policy, which apply alongside this page. During checkout you are transacting with Payhip, and their practices apply to the data they collect from you.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on a third-party platform (Cloudflare). As with most hosts, the platform may automatically record routine access data — such as IP address, browser user agent, requested page, and timestamp — in server and security logs. Those logs are used for basic operations, abuse prevention, and aggregate traffic reporting. We do not merge them with any personal profile, and we do not use them to identify individual visitors.

External links and third parties

Some pages link to resources hosted elsewhere, such as payment pages or related services. Once you follow such a link you leave this site, and the destination's own terms and privacy practices apply. We are not responsible for how third-party sites handle data.

Your choices

You can manage cookies and personalized advertising through your browser settings and Google's ad settings. If you have a question or request about privacy on this website, you can use the feedback channel linked from the site. Because the tools do not send us personal data, there may be little or no personal information on our side to act on.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as the site or the services it uses change. We will reflect any material changes here, and continuing to use the site after an update means you accept the revised version. Last updated: 2026.

Contact

For purchase questions — orders, downloads, or refunds — use the order support channel shown on the relevant product's Payhip page, since the transaction lives there. For questions or problems about the website itself, use the general feedback route offered on the site. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through unencrypted channels.
