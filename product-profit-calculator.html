Use the result before launching a product, changing suppliers, or testing a new sales channel. A product can have a healthy-looking price while still producing a weak margin once every per-sale cost is included.

The calculator reports profit per unit and the total result at your expected sales volume. This lets you compare products that may have different prices, costs, and fee structures.

Start with the selling price and direct product cost per unit. Then account for percentage fees, shipping or fulfillment, and any fixed cost you allocate to each unit.

How to Calculate Product Profitability

What affects product profitability

  • Selling price
  • Unit product cost
  • Marketplace or payment percentage fees
  • Shipping, fulfillment, and allocated fixed costs

How this calculator works

Profit per unit = selling price − product cost − percentage fees − shipping/fulfillment − allocated fixed cost

Example

At 100 units sold for $24, with $8 product cost, $2 shipping, 8% fees, and $1 allocated fixed cost, estimated profit is $11.08/unit and $1,108 total profit, with a margin of about 46.2%.

Important assumptions

Results are planning estimates. Replace the default assumptions with your actual costs, rates, shipping arrangements, discounts, and sales volume. Marketplace and payment fees can change over time.

What is product profit per unit?

It is the selling price minus the product costs and other per-unit costs included in the calculator. It shows how much one sale contributes after those inputs.

How can I improve product profit?

Test the levers you control: pricing, unit cost, fulfillment cost, fee structure, and fixed-cost allocation. Compare scenarios rather than changing several assumptions at once.

Can I use this for my own business?

Yes. Enter your own numbers to create a simple planning estimate. The calculators are designed for comparison and everyday decisions.

Are these official marketplace calculators?

No. They are independent planning tools and do not represent an official marketplace or payment provider.
