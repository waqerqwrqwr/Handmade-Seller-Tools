BEFORE YOU BUY

Why a workbook instead of a free calculator

The free calculators on this site are built for a quick, single answer. They are fast, and they stay open in a browser tab, but each one answers one question at a time and forgets what you told it. A workbook is different in one important way: it keeps your numbers.

If you sell more than a couple of items, you end up pricing the same product again and again as material costs move, as you test a discount, and as a wholesale buyer asks for a trade rate. Doing that in a fresh browser tab means entering the same costs every time. The workbook stores your product data once and reuses it, so the second pricing decision takes far less effort than the first.

The trade-off is real: a workbook is a file you download and manage, not a page you open. If a single one-off calculation is all you need, the free tools are the lighter choice.

What it does not do

This tool is easier to describe by its limits than by its promises.

    Not an accounting systemIt does not track actual sales, produce invoices, or keep books. Use it for pricing decisions, then record transactions where you normally would.
    Not connected to your shopIt does not import orders or sync with a marketplace. The fees you enter are your own assumptions and need updating as rates change.
    Not an automatic cost finderIt cannot know what your materials or your time are worth. Every number in a result comes from something you typed.
    Not a guarantee of profitA price that hits your target margin is still only a plan. Real profit depends on whether the item sells and what it costs to fulfil.

It also does not decide what you should charge; it lays the numbers out so the decision is made with the costs in front of you.

What you need to use it

  • A spreadsheet program that can open a standard workbook file, on a desktop or laptop computer.
  • Your own figures: material costs, the value you place on your time, packaging, and the shipping you pay.
  • Your current selling and marketplace fee assumptions, taken from your own account rather than assumed.
  • A few minutes to enter the first product; later products reuse what you have recorded.

No add-ons, plugins, or subscriptions are required to open or edit the file.

Frequently asked questions

Why pay for a workbook when calculators are free?

The free tools answer one question at a time. The workbook remembers your product data, so repeat pricing does not mean repeat data entry. If you rarely price the same item twice, a free calculator is enough.

Do the screenshots show the real workbook?

Yes. They are views of the actual sheets, not illustrations. Use them to judge whether the layout matches how you like to work before you buy.

What if my costs change after I enter them?

Edit the relevant cells and the views that depend on them update. That is the main reason to keep product data in one place.

Can I share the file with someone else?

It is intended for your own business use. If you work with a partner on the same shop, you can each use your own copy.
