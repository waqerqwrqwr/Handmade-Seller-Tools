How it works

Profit margin = (revenue − total cost) ÷ revenue × 100; markup = profit ÷ cost × 100

Example

If revenue is $5,000 and total costs are $3,250, profit is $1,750 and profit margin is 35%. Markup on cost is about 53.8%. These are different percentages because margin uses revenue as the denominator while markup uses cost.

Important assumptions

Use your own costs and rates whenever possible. Results are estimates for planning and comparison, not financial, tax, legal, or marketplace advice.
