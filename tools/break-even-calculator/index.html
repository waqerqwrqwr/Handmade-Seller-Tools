Break-even assumes a constant selling price and variable cost per unit.

What is a break-even point?

The break-even point is the level of sales where contribution from each unit covers fixed costs.

Break-even formula

Contribution per Unit = Selling Price − Variable Cost
Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution per Unit

Example

If fixed costs are $1,000, price is $30, and variable cost is $18, contribution is $12 per unit. The break-even point is 83.34 units, so 84 whole units are needed to cross it.
