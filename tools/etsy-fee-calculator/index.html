What does this Etsy fee calculator include?

It estimates common sale-related costs: transaction fee, payment processing, listing fee, and an optional Offsite Ads fee. Etsy says its 6.5% transaction fee applies to the order amount, including shipping and gift wrap when charged.

Etsy fee formula

Estimated fees = transaction + processing + listing + optional Offsite Ads

Why your real payout may differ

  • Payment processing rates depend on the seller’s country.
  • Taxes, currency conversion, ads, shipping labels, and other services can add charges.
  • Offsite Ads fees apply only to qualifying orders.
