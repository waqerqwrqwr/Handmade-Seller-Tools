What is an Etsy profit calculator?

This calculator estimates what an Etsy order may leave after marketplace fees and your product and fulfillment costs. It is useful for pricing decisions because revenue alone does not show what you keep.

Etsy profit formula

Profit = order revenue − Etsy fees − product costs − fulfillment costs

Costs worth tracking

  • Materials or cost of goods sold
  • Labor and packaging
  • Shipping you pay
  • Etsy transaction and payment processing fees
  • Listing and optional Offsite Ads fees
  • Other per-order expenses
Etsy’s U.S. example rates currently include a 6.5% transaction fee, $0.20 listing fee, and 3% + $0.25 Etsy Payments processing. Processing rates vary by seller country. See Etsy’s official fee information →
