Markup is based on cost; margin is based on selling price.

Markup vs. margin

Markup answers how much above cost you are charging. Margin answers how much of the selling price is profit. A 50% markup produces a 33.3% margin.

Markup formula

Selling Price = Cost × (1 + Markup ÷ 100)

Example

With a $20 cost and a 50% markup, the selling price is $30 and profit is $10.
