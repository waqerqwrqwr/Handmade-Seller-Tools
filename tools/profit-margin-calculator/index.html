What is profit margin?

Profit margin shows how much of your revenue remains as profit after the costs you include. A 30% margin means $30 of profit for every $100 of revenue, before costs you have not included.

Profit margin formula

Profit = Revenue − Total Costs
Profit Margin = Profit ÷ Revenue × 100

Margin vs. markup

Margin is measured against the selling price. Markup is measured against cost. They are related, but they are not the same number. Use the Markup Calculator → when pricing starts with cost.
