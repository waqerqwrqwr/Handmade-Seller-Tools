How to Set a Wholesale Price

Wholesale pricing checks

  • Retail price and wholesale discount
  • Unit production or sourcing cost
  • Packaging and seller-paid shipping
  • Order quantity and resulting total profit

How this calculator works

Wholesale price = retail price × (1 − wholesale discount); profit = wholesale price − unit cost − packaging − seller-paid shipping

Example

At a $40 retail price with a 50% wholesale discount, $16 unit cost, $1 packaging, and no seller-paid shipping, wholesale price is $20, profit is $3/unit, and margin is 15%.

Important assumptions

Results are planning estimates. Replace the default assumptions with your actual costs, rates, shipping arrangements, discounts, and sales volume. Marketplace and payment fees can change over time.

How do I calculate a wholesale price?

One common approach is to start from the retail price and apply a wholesale discount, then confirm that the result still covers your unit costs and desired margin.

Should shipping be included in wholesale pricing?

Include shipping when you are the party paying it. If the buyer pays shipping separately, do not treat that shipping amount as your per-unit cost.

Can I use this for my own business?

Yes. Enter your own numbers to create a simple planning estimate. The calculators are designed for comparison and everyday decisions.
