How it works

Wholesale price = retail price × (1 − wholesale discount); unit profit = wholesale price − total unit cost

Example

Suppose your retail price is $40 and you offer retailers a 50% wholesale discount. The wholesale price is $20 per unit. If unit cost is $16, packaging is $1, and shipping is $0, total unit cost is $17. That leaves $3 profit per unit, or a 15% wholesale margin.

Important assumptions

Use your own costs and rates whenever possible. Results are estimates for planning and comparison, not financial, tax, legal, or marketplace advice.

It uses the inputs you provide to estimate the selected pricing, fee, profit, margin, or break-even result. Change the assumptions to match your own business.
